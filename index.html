How goal pacing works

The rule is applied once a day, after the previous day closes.

Spend less than your daily cap → the unused amount is added to the primary goal. Spend more than your daily cap → the excess is deducted from the primary goal. The current day stays live; its final adjustment happens automatically after midnight.
